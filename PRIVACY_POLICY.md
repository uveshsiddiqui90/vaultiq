# Privacy Policy — VaultIQ

**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

VaultIQ ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information**
- Email address
- Password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)
- Full name
- Profile picture (optional)

**Financial Data**
- Budget amounts
- Expense entries (amount, category, date, notes)
- Transaction history

**Device Information**
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- Unique device identifier (for internal use only)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Data**
- Features you access
- Time and duration of use
- Actions performed in the app

**Technical Data**
- IP address
- Device performance metrics
- Crash reports and errors

We use the collected information to:

✅ **Provide Services**
- Create and manage your account

- Display analytics and reports

✅ **Improve the App**
- Analyze usage patterns
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Develop new features

✅ **Security & Compliance**
- Detect fraudulent activity

✅ **Communication**
- Send password resets

## 4. Data Security

### 4.1 Encryption & Protection

- All data transmitted between app and server uses **SSL/TLS encryption**
- Passwords are **hashed and salted** using industry-standard algorithms
- Sensitive data at rest is **encrypted** on Supabase servers
- No financial data is stored on your device without encryption

### 4.2 Access Control

- Only authorized personnel can access your data
- Access is limited to what's necessary for job function
- All access is logged and monitored

### 4.3 Data Retention

- Account data is retained as long as your account is active
- Expense records are retained for 7 years (for financial/tax records)
- Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days
- You can request data deletion anytime (account will be deactivated)

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 Access Your Data

You have the right to request and download all personal data we hold about you in a structured, portable format.

### 6.2 Update Your Information

You can update your profile information, email, and name anytime in the app.

### 6.3 Delete Your Account

You can request complete account deletion:
1. Go to Profile → Settings
2. Tap "Delete Account"
3. Confirm deletion
4. Your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days

All personal data will be erased except what we're legally required to keep (tax records for 7 years).

### 6.4 Opt-Out Options

- **Notifications**: Turn off in app settings
- **Analytics**: Disable in privacy settings
- **Email Communications**: Unsubscribe from email footer

## 7. Children's Privacy

VaultIQ is **not intended for children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will promptly delete the information and the account.

Parents/guardians who believe a child under 13 has provided information can contact us immediately.
